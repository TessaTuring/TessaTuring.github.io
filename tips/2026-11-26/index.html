Screenshot a sale before you tap Buy

Take a screenshot of the deal and share it with ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Ask for the total cost with shipping, the return window and the small print. Found it in two places? Upload both and get a simple side-by-side table.

The problem

Sale pages are built to hurry you

Countdown clocks, big red prices, tiny print. One screenshot and one question to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini shows you the full picture before you pay.

Step 1

Take a screenshot of the deal

Capture the price, the shipping line and the return details if you can see them. In the AI app, tap the plus or paperclip icon, then choose your screenshot.

Step 2

Ask for the full picture

Send this along with your screenshot. Fill in the brackets.

I'm thinking of buying this for [what I need it for]. From this screenshot, list the total cost including shipping, the return window, what's included, and anything in the small print I should check before buying.
Step 3

Compare two offers side by side

Saw it somewhere else too? Upload both screenshots in the same message.

Here are two offers for [item]. Put them in a simple table: price, shipping, delivery date, return policy and warranty. Then tell me which details you couldn't see so I can check them on each store's website.
Stay safe

Got the deal by text or email?

Ask the AI if anything looks off, then go to the store's official website by typing the address yourself. Keep card numbers and passwords out of the chat.
