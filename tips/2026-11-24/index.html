Don't know what to ask AI? Let it ask you

Try this: tell it what you need help with, then ask it to ask YOU a few questions first. It asks one question at a time, and you can tap the microphone and answer out loud. When it's done, ask it to list what it assumed so you know what to double-check.

The blank box

Not sure what to type? That's normal.

Many people freeze at the empty chat box. A simple fix: say what you want help with, then ask the AI to ask you a few questions before it answers.

Copy this

The ask-me-first prompt

Works in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Meta AI. Fill in the brackets with your own task.

I want help with [what you need, for example planning my parents' holiday visit]. Before you answer, ask me up to 5 questions, one at a time, so you understand my situation. Then give me your suggestion in plain English.
Easy answers

Tap the microphone and just talk

In ChatGPT, tap the microphone icon next to the typing box and speak your answer. Short answers are fine. Saying I'm not sure is a fine answer too.

Before and after

Example: a birthday card for your sister

Asked plainly, the AI writes a card that could suit anyone. Asked to question you first, it learns her age, a shared memory and your tone. The card sounds like the two of you.

Last step

Ask it what to double-check

Paste this once it gives you its answer. It shows you any guesses it made along the way.

Thank you. Now list anything you assumed about my situation, and anything I should double-check before I use this.
Stay safe

Keep private details out of the chat

Answer its questions in general terms. Leave out account numbers, passwords and ID numbers. Saying my bank or my mum's doctor gives it plenty to work with.
