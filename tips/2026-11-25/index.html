That confusing letter? Snap a photo and ask

Take a photo of it (cover your account and ID numbers first) and upload it to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Ask for a plain-English summary: what it is about, what you need to do, and any deadlines. Then ask which parts to confirm, and get a few polite questions ready for your call.

The problem

Official letters can feel like a puzzle

Insurance notices, benefit letters, billing statements. The one thing you need to do is often buried on page two. A photo and a short prompt can pull it out for you.

Before you snap

Cover your private details first

Fold over or cover account numbers, ID numbers and your signature, then take the photo. The AI only needs the wording to explain the letter.

How to upload

Tap the plus or paperclip icon

In ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, tap the plus or paperclip icon next to the message box, choose Camera or Photos, and add your picture.

Prompt 1

Get the plain-English version

Paste this in with your photo. Fill in the part in brackets.

Here is a photo of a letter from [who sent it]. Explain it in plain, everyday English. Then list: 1. What it is about 2. What, if anything, I need to do 3. Any dates or deadlines 4. Any tricky words, with simple meanings.
Prompt 2

Ask what to double-check

Send this right after the first answer, in the same chat.

Which parts of your explanation should I confirm with the sender directly? Then write 3 short, polite questions I can ask when I call them about [my main worry or question].
Good to know

The sender has the final word

AI can misread a blurry photo. To confirm anything about money, health or deadlines, call the sender using the number on your card or their official website.
