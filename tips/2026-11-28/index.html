Turn that old family recipe card into one you can use

Take a photo of the card and upload it to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Ask it to type the card up exactly, then update old measurements and scale it for your crowd. Last step: ask what to double-check, so you know which guesses to confirm with family or test first.

The problem

That faded recipe card in the drawer

Family recipes are often faded, use old measurements and skip steps the cook just knew. A phone photo and three short prompts can turn one into a clear recipe the whole family can follow.

Step 1

Take a clear photo of the card

Lay the card flat near a window so the writing is easy to see. In ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, tap the plus or paperclip icon, choose your photo, then paste the prompt on the next slide.

Prompt 1: type it up

Get the card typed exactly as written

Send this with your photo. Fill in the parts in square brackets.

This is a photo of my [grandmother's] handwritten recipe for [dish]. Please type it up exactly as written. Where a word or amount is hard to read, tell me and give your best guess. Then list any steps the card seems to leave out.
Prompt 2: make it usable

Update the measurements and scale it

Send this next in the same chat so it remembers the recipe.

Now rewrite this as a clear, numbered recipe for [number] people. Change old terms like a moderate oven or a teacup of flour into today's amounts and oven temperatures in [Fahrenheit or Celsius]. Next to each change, show the original wording so nothing is lost.
Prompt 3: check it

Ask what to double-check before you cook

Old recipes can hide guesses. This prompt brings them to the surface.

What in this recipe should I double-check with a relative or test on a small batch first? Point out every guess you made, any amounts or timings that look unusual, and anything a first-timer might get wrong.
Tip

Share it so the family can fill the gaps

Ask the AI to lay the recipe out on one page for printing, then send it to the family group chat. Someone may remember the missing step only they knew.
