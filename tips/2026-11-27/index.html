5 AI tools worth trying if you're a grandparent

Meta AI lives inside WhatsApp, so help is one chat away. Google Lens answers the endless what-is-that questions on walks. Khanmigo, Canva and ChatGPT cover homework help, party flyers and visit plans.

Meta AI

Get help right inside your WhatsApp family chat

Open a chat with Meta AI in WhatsApp and ask for a warm birthday message for a grandchild, or a fun question to post in the family group to get everyone talking.

Google Lens

Answer the grandkids' what-is-that questions

On a walk, point your phone camera at a leaf, bug or flower and Lens suggests what it is. It can also translate a sign or a label when you travel together.

Khanmigo

Help with homework without doing it for them

When a grandchild is stuck on homework during a visit, sit together with Khan Academy's AI tutor and let it coach them through the question while you cheer them on.

Canva

Make a flyer for a family party or reunion

Pick a flyer design, type the date, place and what to bring, and add a favorite photo. Print it for the fridge or share it in the family chat.

ChatGPT

Plan a visit full of things to do together

Share the grandkids' ages, what they love and what supplies you have, then ask for easy activities. Tap the microphone icon to say it out loud if typing feels slow.

Try this today

Plan a grandkid visit in one prompt

Works in ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Leave out full names, school names and home addresses.

My grandchildren are [ages] and love [interests]. They're visiting for [how long] and I have [space or supplies]. Before you plan, ask me 3 questions. Then give an easy plan: one indoor and one outdoor idea per day, plus anything to check for safety.
