Behind on the family group chat? Try this

Take a few screenshots of what you missed and upload them to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. Ask for a plain-English catch-up: what was decided, what is still open, and anything asked of you. Then have it draft a short, friendly reply you can paste straight in.

Sound familiar?

You open the family chat and it is miles long

Holiday plans, a dozen photos, three side conversations, and somewhere in there a question meant for you. Scrolling back takes ages. A few screenshots can do the catching up for you.

Step 1

Take 2 or 3 screenshots of what you missed

Scroll up to where you left off and screenshot down to the latest message. Crop out any addresses or account numbers. In ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini, tap the plus or paperclip to add them.

Step 2

Ask for a plain-English catch-up

Paste this under your screenshots and fill in your name. It pulls out the bits that matter to you.

These screenshots are from my family group chat. Catch me up in plain English: 1) what has been decided, 2) what is still undecided, 3) any question asked of me, [your name], or anything I am expected to bring or do. Keep it short.
Step 3

Get a friendly reply ready to paste

Tell it your answer in a few words. It writes the message, and you copy it into the family chat.

Now write one short, friendly reply I can paste into the chat. My answer is: [for example, I can bring the apple pie and get there around 2]. Keep it warm and casual, under 3 sentences, and answer anyone who asked me something by name.
Tip

Prefer to talk? Tap the microphone

Tap the microphone icon in the message box and say your answer out loud. The AI types it for you. Read the reply once before sending, and check any dates or times against the chat.
